Brief Summary  

PURPOSE OF THE STUDY: Regarding the time of administration of Niruha Basti, it is said to be given to a person who is not too hungry after the Jeernanna Lakshanas are observed which is usually seen in Pratah Kala. Also, the person should have voided urine and faeces. There is another opinion that it should be administered in Madhyahna Kala, specifically Madhyahne Kinjith Avarthe which means some time after the commencement of Madhyahna. So a need has been felt, to find out whether Niruha Basti Pranidhana Kala is significant while evaluating the effect of Basti in a disease and does it have any influence on the assessment of Samyak Niruda Lakshanas. So the present study is planned to evaluate the effect of different Pranidhana Kala of Niruha Basti.

AIM: To assess the variation of response to Erandamooladi Niruha Basti in Gridrasi depending on time of administration


OBJECTIVES OF THE STUDY:

1)To assess the variation of response to Erandamooladi Niruha Basti administered as Yoga basti in Gridrasi in Madhyahna Kala

2)To assess the variation of response to Erandamooladi Niruha Basti administered as Yoga basti in Gridrasi in Pratah Kala

3)To compare the response to Erandamooladi Niruha Basti administered as Yoga basti in Gridrasi in Madhyahna Kala and Pratah Kala


MATERIALS AND METHODS: Open label double armed, randomised, clinical trial with appropriate sample size conducted in Amrita School of Ayurveda. Pre-diagnosed patients of Gridrasi fulfilling inclusion criteria will be taken for study and randomly divided into 2 groups namely Group Madhyahna Kala (GMK) and Group Pratah Kala (GPK). In both groups, the patients will receive Sthanika Abhyanga on Udara and Prishta regions for 5 minutes with Sahacharadi Thaila followed by Nadisweda with Ushnajala before administration of Niruha Basti. The time of administration of Basti will be recorded daily. After Pratyagamana of Basti Dravya, the patient is advised to take Ushnajala Snana followed by intake of rice gruel. Anuvasana Basti will be administered soon after the lunch.

Group GMK: The patients will be given light breakfast (3 Idli / Dosa with tea or the equal quantity of other materials) in the morning at 8.00 am. Niruha Basti will be administered in Madhyahna Kala (between 11.30 am to 12.30 pm) after Jeernahara Lakshanas are observed when the patient is not too hungry as per the schedule.

Group GPK: Niruha Basti will be administered in the morning hours (between 7.30am to 8.30 am) on the empty stomach when the patient is not too hungry and Jeernahara Lakshanas are observed.

In both groups, the patients will receive Yoga basti with Sahacharadi Thaila Anuvasana Basti and Erandamooladi Niruha Basti. The patients will receive Yoga basti with Sahacharadi Thaila Anuvasana Basti and Erandamooladi Niruha Basti.

ASSESSMENT CRITERIA: The assessment will be done at the baseline and after treatment based on the clinical changes observed in the patients. Self-gradation   will be done for the assessment parameters. Subjective Parameters: Samyak Basti Lakshanas, Improvement in the signs and symptoms of Gridrasi, Visual analogue scale for pain (numeric rating scale), SLR test and Roland-Morris Disability Scale. Objective Parameters: Range of movement of the lumbar spine with the goniometer, walking time, walking distance and standing time.
