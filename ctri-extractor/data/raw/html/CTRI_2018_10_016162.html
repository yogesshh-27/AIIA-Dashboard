Brief Summary  

This is an open label randomised single centred trial comparing efficacy of ayurvedic drugs, Kamdugha Rasa with Dhanyaka hima and Jatamansi Phanta in the managementof Amlapitta. Patients of both the sexes between age 15 to 75 years of Amlapitta will be selected for relieving sign and symptoms. 100 clinically diagnosed and confirmed patients will be randomly divided in to two groups for administration of trial drugs. In group A 50 patients will be administered  trial drug Kamdugha rasa  250 mg with dhanyaka hima 100 ml BD orally for 21 days. In group B 50 patients will be administered  trial drug Kamdugha rasa  250 mg with Jatamansi phanta 100 ml BD orally for 21 days.

All the patients registered for clinical trial will be asked for any changes in their clinical menifestations and growing feeling of well being. Conclusion- To compare the efficacy of both the groups
