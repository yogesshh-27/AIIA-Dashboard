Publication Details    
Brief Summary   The study is a randomised parallel clinical study to evaluate the efficacy of smavardhana ghrita along with yoga basti in one group we have taken samvardhana ghrita orally for 62 days and udvarthan abhyanga swedana for 10days  while in other group samvardhan ghrita with yoga basti later group udvarthana rubbing body skin with barley and horse gram flour for five days abhyanga massage by medicated oil named balataila for five days then course of yoga bast total 8 ayurvedic medicated enema this whole procedure from udvarthana to basti will be repeated for total 3 times in that manner in that gap between each course
