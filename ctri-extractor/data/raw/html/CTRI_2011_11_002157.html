Brief Summary
Modification(s)  		 In both the group we have taken Udvartana with Yava & Kulattha for 5 days
Abhyanga with luke warm Bala Taila for 5 days as preoperative procedures..
which is followed by main procedure which is under trial i.e. Chaturbhadra kalpa basti in group A and Yoga Basti in group B.

Chaturbhadra Kalpa Basti consist of 4 Anuvasana Basti at first, then 4 Asthapana Basti in middle, and 4 Anuvasan Basti at last....total 12 days

Yoga Basti consist of 1 Anuvasana Basti first then 3 alternative Anuvasana and Asthapana Basti for 6 days, then lastly 1 Anuvasana Basti....total 8 days

Anuvasana Basti is kind of enima in which medicated oil is inserted through anal rout...we have taken Bala Taila for Anuvasana

Asthapana Basti is also given through rectal rout with mixture of Honey, Saindhav (rock salt), Sneha (here Bala Taila), Kalka (here Shata Pushpa, Madanphala), Kwatha (here Eranda mula decoction)

In both the group internal medicine was given i.e. Ashtanga Ghrita.

Patients were Assessed before and after treatment with the objective parameters like..
1. MAS for Spasticity,
2. Muscle power scale,
3. Reflexia scale for DTR,
4. Goniometry for passive range of motion,
5. CDC grading for gross and fine motor development.
