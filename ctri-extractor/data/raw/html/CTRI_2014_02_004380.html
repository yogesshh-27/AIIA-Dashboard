Therefore, it is concluded that Pasanabhedadi Ghrita  possesses the properties to disintegrate and expel the Ashmari (urinary stones) from the urinary tract and provided relief in signs and symptoms of Ashmari (Urolithiasis) comprehensively without producing any adverse effect to the patients.
