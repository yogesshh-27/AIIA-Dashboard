Brief Summary  

This study is Randomized parallel clinical study to evaluate the effect of Badara ointment and Ingudi ointment  in Vyanga. Routine blood examination  will be performed to rule out other systematic pathology. Special scoring scale has been

opted based on signs and symptoms given in Ayurvedic classical texts which will be utilized for assessment and evalution. Ponds color grading scale is used to assess the  discoloration on face.
