Brief Summary  

The first trimester of pregnancy lasts from conception until 12 weeks gestation. Women experience a wide range of symptoms as their bodies undergo some amazing 

changes during the first trimester. Some symptoms during this stage include 

  Nausea and vomiting

  Fatigue

  Food aversion

  Breast growth and tenderness                                                                                                                                   

  Tiredness

  Constipation

  Regurgitation and heartburn

  Lower abdominal pain

 Frequency micturation etc.

In Group A rasayana granules 10gm BD for 2 months will be given.

In Group B micronized progesteron 200mg BD will be given for 2 months.
