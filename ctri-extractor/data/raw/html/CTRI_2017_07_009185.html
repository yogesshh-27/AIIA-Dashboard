Type  Name  Details 
Intervention  Group A -Vamana Karma followed by Shamana yoga.  Poorvakarma: Drug for Deepana Pachana: Panchakola Choorna 3 gms twice a day before food till Nirama Lakshanas are obtained. Shodhananga Snehapana: Moorchita Go Ghrita for 3 to 7 days, as per Koshtha or till Samyaka Snigdha Lakshana are obtained. Sarvanga Abhayanga : Dashamoola Tailam & Mridu Sarvanga Sveda (Bashpa Sveda) for 2 days. Pradhana Karma: Vamaka Yoga: 5 to 6 grams of Madana phalapippali churna with other conventional drugs like Yastimadhu (Glycerazzhia glabra), Rock salt and Honey. Pashchat Karma: According to Shuddi Samsarjana Krama is advised for 3 to 7 days. Shamana yoga: Deepaniya Mahakashaya Ghana Vati is given for 30 days in the dosage of 1gm (2 pills each of 500 mg) for 3 times a day before food with warm water. 
Intervention  Group B Basti Karma followed by Shamana Yoga  Koshatakyadi Kaphanashaka Basti for 16 days as per Kala Basti schedule .In this schedule first two days Anuvasana Basti is given then alternate six Niruha and six Anuvasana is given andat last two anuvasana are given. Poorvakarma The patient is subjected to Sarvanga Abhyanga with Dashamoola Taila followed by Bashpa Svedana. For Anuvasana Basti, the patient is asked to have diet in lesser quantity than regular consumption, attend natural urges and walk a few steps before reaching the Basti room. On the day of Niruha Basti patient is asked to come in empty stomach. Pradhanakarma: Administration of Anuvasana Basti: with Dashamoola taila in a dose of 60 ml, is given after regular diet. Administration of Niruha Basti: Koshatakyadi kaphanashaka Basti. Total Dose: 480 ml which contains Madhu-80 gms Saindhava Lavana-5gms Sneham-Tila taila and sarshapataila 60 ml each (Total 120 ml) Kalkam- 40 gms Kwatham- 240 ml Yavaksharam- 4gm. The observations like time of administration of Basti Dravya, time of expulsion and Samyaka Lakshana, Vyapad if any and vitals is noted on each day. Pashchat Karma: On the day of Anuvasana Basti light food is advised after 9 hours of Basti administration or after the Pratyagamana. On the day of Niruha Basti patients are advised to take light warm liquid food like green gram soup, rice, dal, daliya after Basti Pratyagamana (the expulsion of administered Niruha Basti dravya). Shamana yoga Deepaniya Mahakashaya Ghana Vati is given for 30 days in the dosage of 1gm (2 pills each of 500 mg) for 3 times a day before food with warm water.  
Comparator Agent  Group C Control Group  Tab Thyroxine is given as per the prescribed dose empty stomach in the morning in 30 patients for 45 days.

Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

The trial is a standard controlled study in hypothyroidism compared with Dhatwagnimandya (decreased digestive fire) with three groups.  Diagnosed cases of Hypothyroidism are registered in the trial and taken randomly in the three groups of 30 patients each In Group A ,30 patients are being recruited for the procedure of Vamana Karma (therapeutic emesis) followed by shamana yoga (oral medicine) .The process will be as follows   Poorvakarma:(pre preparatory procedure) Drug for Deepana Pachana: Panchakola Choorna 3 gms twice a day before food till Nirama Lakshanas are obtained. Shodhananga  Snehapana: Moorchita Go Ghrita for 3 to 7 days, as per Koshtha or till Samyaka Snigdha Lakshana are obtained. Sarvanga Abhayanga : Dashamoola Tailam & Mridu Sarvanga Sveda (Bashpa Sveda)  for 2 days. Pradhana Karma(main procedure)   Vamaka Yoga: 5 to 6 grams of Madana phalapippali churna with other conventional drugs like Yastimadhu (Glycerazzhia glabra), Rock salt and Honey.   Pashchat Karma: (post procedure) :According to Shuddi Samsarjana Krama is advised for 3 to 7 days. Shamana yoga: Deepaniya Mahakashaya Ghana Vati is given for 30 days in the dosage of 1gm (2 pills each of 500 mg) for 3 times a day before food with warm water. In Group B , 30  patients are being recruited for the procedure of koshatakyadi Kaphanashak Basti (Therapeutic Enema) for 16 days followed by  Shamana Aushadi .The process willbe as follows Poorvakarma  The patient is subjected to Sarvanga Abhyanga with Dashamoola Taila followed by Bashpa Svedana. For Anuvasana Basti, the patient is asked to have diet in lesser quantity than regular consumption, attend natural urges and walk a few steps before reaching the Basti room. On the day of Niruha Basti patient is asked to come in empty stomach. Pradhanakarma:  Administration of Anuvasana Basti: with Dashamoola taila in a dose of 60 ml, is given after regular diet.   Administration of Niruha Basti: Koshatakyadi kaphanashaka Basti. Total Dose: 480 ml which contains Madhu-80 gms Saindhava Lavana-5gms Sneham-Tila taila and sarshapataila 60 ml each (Total 120 ml) Kalkam- 40 gms  Kwatham- 240 ml Yavaksharam- 4gm.  The observations like time of administration of Basti Dravya, time of expulsion and Samyaka Lakshana, Vyapad if any and vitals  is noted on each day. Pashchat Karma: On the day of Anuvasana Basti light food is advised after 9 hours of Basti administration or after the Pratyagamana. On the day of Niruha Basti patients are advised to take light warm liquid food like green gram soup, rice, dal, daliya after Basti Pratyagamana (the expulsion of administered Niruha Basti dravya).  Shamana yoga Deepaniya Mahakashaya Ghana Vati  is given for 30 days in the dosage of 1gm (2 pills each of 500 mg) for 3 times a day before food with warm water.In Group C ,Tab Thyroxine for duration of 45 days will be given as per the prescribed dose empty stomach in the morning.T3,T4,TSH will be done three times before treatment,then after Vamana Karma procedure or Basti Karma  procedure and third after Shamana Aushadi i.e Deepaniya Mahakashaya Ghana vati. total duration of the trial is 45 days.Group C is comparitive group.
