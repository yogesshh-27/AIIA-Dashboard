Brief Summary  

This study is an  open labelled single arm clinical trial to evaluate the effect of Vasa Khanda Kushmanda Avaleha in Amlapitta . It is done with pre and post-test design for the included 30 patients. Here 6 gm of Vasa Khanda Kushmanda Avaleha  will be administered four times daily after food for 15 days . The patients would be assessed on the 7th day and on the 15th day of the treatment with investigations at the beginning and the end of the treatment. Assessment would be done based on subjective and objective parameters and would also be monitored for Adverse Drug Reactions or Adverse Drug Effects or other concurrent illness.
