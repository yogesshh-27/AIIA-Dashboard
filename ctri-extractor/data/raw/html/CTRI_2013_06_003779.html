Brief Summary   This study is Randomized placebo control clinical study to evaluate the efficacy of Pippalimula on the Patients suffering from Ama. Routine blood, urine and stool examinations will be carried out to assess the status of Ama and also to rule out any other systemic pathology. Special scoring scale has been prepared based on the information in Ayurvedic classics which will be utilized for assessment and evaluation.
