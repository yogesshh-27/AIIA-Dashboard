Brief Summary
Modification(s)  
This study is randomized parallel clinical study to evaluate and compare effect of Lifestyle modification and Shatapushpadya churna containing Shatapushpa, Maricha, Vidanga and Saindhava in Agnimandya (Indigestion) patients. Routine blood, urine and stool examination will be performed to rule out any other systemic pathology. Special scoring and gradation pattern has been preapared based on the information available in Ayurvedic classical texts. It will be utilized for evaluation and effect. Assessment of Agni will be carried out as per Ayurvedic method.
