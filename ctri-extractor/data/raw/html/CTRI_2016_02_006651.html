Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

The main aim of present study was to assess the effect of Guduchighana on Panduroga.  The study was designed as Single Arm Clinical trial. The study had obtained Institutional Ethics Committee clearance (PGT/7/-A/Ethics/2015-16/1470, 25/08/2015) and is registered at Clinical Trial Registry of India, ICMR, New Delhi, vide CTRI/2016/02/006651. Four tablets of Guduchighanavati (250 mg each), two times daily at Nirannakala for one month along with water as Anupana was given.Overall Effect of Therapy on Various Parameters: On cardinal features 78.16% relief  was found (40.59%), on associated features 90.49% relief was found, on heamatological parameters 1.92% was found and on bio- marker 0.84% relief was found. Overall Effect of Therapy: In present study, 20% patients had moderate improvement and 76.66% patients had mild improvement, 3.33% patients remain unchanged after treatment. Completely cured or marked improvement was not found in any patient.
