Brief Summary   This study is open clinical trial to evaluate the efficacy of langhana karma (fasting) in patients suffering from ama(indigestion). Routine blood, urine and stool examination will be performed to rule out other systemic pathology. Special scoring scale has been prepared based on information in ayurvedic classical texts which will be utilized for assessment and evaluation. Saama niraama condition of stool will be assessed as per Ayurveda.
