Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

This study was a Randomized parallel clinical trial carried out to evaluate and compare the effect of Haritaki (Terminalia chebula Retz.) and Sauvarchala lavana (unaqua sodium chloride) given in one group and Haritaki (single drug) given in other group in the patients having complaint of Purishaja Anaha (Constipation). Routine blood, urine and stool investigations were performed to rule out any other systemic pathology. Special scoring and gradation scale had been prepared based on information in Ayurvedic classical texts which was utilized for assessment and evaluation. The final results showed that in the patients suffering from Purishaja Anaha (Constipation) when the drug Haritaki (Terminalia chebula Retz.) was administered along with Sauvarchala Lavana (unaqua sodium chloride) showed better results as compared to single Haritaki administration in this disease.
