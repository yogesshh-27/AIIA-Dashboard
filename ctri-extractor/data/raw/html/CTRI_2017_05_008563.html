Brief Summary  

Hypothesis-Medhya Rasayana is effective in the management of Dementia of the Alzheimerâ€™s type.

Dementia is one of the major causes of disability and dependency among older people worldwide. The impact of dementia on caregivers, family and societies can be physical, psychological, social and economical. With the modern therapeutics, though there is spectacular achievement in providing relief in the patients of dementia, still a high risk exists due to serious side effects of therapy. Recently there is tremendous urge to explore medicinal plants globally for improving cognitive function owing to their less adverse effects. These drugs are having Aayuprada, Aamayanashana, Bala, Agni, Varna, Swara vardhaka and Medhya properties, Hence this study is conceptualized using Medhya Rasayana. The present study involves oral administration of Medhya Rasayanas as churna containing Mandukparni, Guduchi, Shankhapushpi and Yashtimadhu. These herbs are been extensively researched for their Nootropic effects. This is a humble effort to evaluate the efficacy of Medhya Rasayana of dementia of the Alzheimerâ€™s type.
