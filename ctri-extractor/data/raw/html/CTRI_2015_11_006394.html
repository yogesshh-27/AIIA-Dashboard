Inclusion Criteria  
Age From  1.00 Day(s)
Age To  28.00 Day(s)
Gender  Both 
Details  A full term delivered; appropriate for gestational age (36 to 40 weeks), healthy babies of either sex weighing between 2.5 kg to 3.5 kg will be included in study. 
 
ExclusionCriteria 
Details  Preterm babies (< 36 weeks gestation), babies with birth weight < 2.5kg and >3.5kg, babies with eventful birth history like perinatal asphyxia, birth asphyxia, IDM, babies of eclamptic or pre-eclamptic mother, babies whose mother having significant antenatal history of illness will be excluded from the study. Babies of hepatitis B or HIV infected mother, baby whose mother suffering from illness (TB or any such active infection) and babies with congenital anomalies like CHD etc. also will be excluded from the study.

Brief Summary
Modification(s)  		 Clinical trial completed successfully with statistically significant improvement in Umbilical Cord Care with Jatyadi Taila and improved neonatal outcome with the Jatamatra Paricharya - Ayurveda Newborn care. Ayurveda newborn care applied was Abhyanga (Neonatal Massage), Umbilical cord care with Jatyadi Taila, Shiropichu with Brahmi Ghrita. There is definite role of Ayurveda in good health outcome, and neonatal care and health.
