Brief Summary  

        Infertility is defined as failure to conceive more than one year of regular & unprotected coitus. It is emotionally involved with a lot of social stigma.

Infertility divided into 2 types:

  1. Primary - patients who have never conceived.
  2. Secondary - Indicates previous proven pregnancy but fails to conceive subsequently.

Correlating fallopian tubes with the Artavavaha (Artava-beeja-vaha) Srotas, its block can be compared as Sanga type of Srotodushti.

Abnormality of tubes is generally of two types:-

(1)Structural - Anatomical blockage - due to vitiation of Vata-Kapha / Pitta Kapha

(2) Functional - defect in cilliary function of tube -due to vitiation of Vata.

Tubal block -30%

The management of infertility due to tubal factor in modern depends upon the site of blockage: includes Tubal Microsurgery, Tubal Adhesiolysis, Fimbrioplasty, I.V.F., Tube re-canalization. But all these methods have its own limits; success rate 50-60%in isthmic block.Moreover all these methods are the solution only for the anatomical aspect of the tube and no success in establishing the tubal function. So it is the time to develop a safe, established, cost effective and permanent cure Ayurvedic protocol for this sensitive problem.

Virechana (Samshodhana therapy) is a biocleansing method meant for cleaning of micro channels (minute Strotas). Fallopian tube can be correlated with micro channel related to Aartavvaha strotas. So any obstruction in fallopian tube can be removed by Virechana Karma. Uttarabasti [Intra Uterine Uttarabasti (IUUB)] with various medicated oil / ghee is an unique procedure mentioned in Ayurvedic classics especially for the treatment of Vandhyatva, Artavadusti and other Yoniroga (All gynecological disorders), where other treatments become failure. So keeping hypothesis in mind for opening of the tube, Apamarga Kshara Taila will be used because of its Lekhana (scraping) property, Phalkalyana Ghrita for Ropana and better function of (cillary movement) fallopian tube and to promote conception.
