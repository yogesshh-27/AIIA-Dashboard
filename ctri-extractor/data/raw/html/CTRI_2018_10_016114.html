Brief Summary   Scholastic backwardness is a common problems among the schoolchildren in rural and urban milieu. In simple terms, it can be termed as securing very low marks in one or more subjects most of the time in most of the examinations.The current study aims to study the efficacy of a treatment model combining Ayurveda, Yoga and Music therapy in managing hyperactive children with Scholastic Backwardness attending an outpatient clinic in comparison with standard management of such children with Individualized education program.The study aims to create an applicable therapeutic and prophylactic model for managing and preventing scholastic backwardness among school going children as well as extend it to the management of all learning disorders in children with hyperactivity.
