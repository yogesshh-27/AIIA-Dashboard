Brief Summary  
The current study is planned to validate the efficacy of Virechana karma and shamana yoga in the management of Sthaulya.

In group A Classical Virechana karma followed by Shamana drug with Pathyapathya

In Group B Vidangadi yoga as Shamana yoga,4 tab of 500 mg thrice a day will be given for 1 month with Pathyapathya as mention in Sthaulya

In Group c Placebo controlled group.
