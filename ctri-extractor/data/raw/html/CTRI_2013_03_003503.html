Brief Summary
Modification(s)  		 the study is to evaluate role of Vamana karma in Group and Amalaki Rasayana in Group B and iron and folic acid in group C.in vamana group,classical vamana was done after undergoing deepan pachan,snehan and swedan.after samsarjana krama,amalaki rasayana was given for 45 days.in group B only amalaki rasayana was given for 45 days while in group C ferrous fumarate and folic acid was given for 45 days.Assessment of therapy was done on the basis of improvement in clinical signs and symptoms of anemia with increase in value of Hb,blood cell indices, serum iron and TIBC.
