Brief Summary   it is a controlled parallel group trial ,45 diagnosed patients of Urolithiasis will be enrolled and randomly devided in three groups.,15   in each group. patients will be diagnosed on the basis of sign and symptoms of urolithiasis. in group A  patients will be treated with 40 ml of varun shigru kwath with prakshep of yavakshar per orally twice a day for 30 days.in  group B  patients will be treated with 40 ml of Ashmarihara kwath per orally twice a day for 30 days.in  group c patients will be treated with 500mg tablet of Ashmarihara kwath ghanvati per orally twice a day for 30 days.after completion of treatment all patients will be followed up for 30 days. the improvement in sign and symptoms of urolithiasis will be assessed after the completion of therapy on the basis of specially designed proforma.
