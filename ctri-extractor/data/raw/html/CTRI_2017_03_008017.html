Brief Summary
Modification(s)  		 The present study is an open labelled comparative clinical study to assess the effectiveness of Salavana Upanaha (poultice containing Wheat powder, Acorus calamus, Embelia ribes, Cedrus deodara, Ricinus communis, Pluchea lanceolata) with and without Parisheka (pouring medicated liquids) on Spasticity in children with cerebral palsy. 60 subjects will be divide into two groups where one group will be administered upanaha along with parisheka and the other with only upanaha (poultice) for  a period of 90 days. The primary outcome measure will be the reduction of spasticity in children with spastic cerebral palsy. The secondary outcome measures will be improvement in the quality of life of the study subjects.
