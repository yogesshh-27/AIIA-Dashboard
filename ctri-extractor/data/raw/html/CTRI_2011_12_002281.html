Brief Summary  

This study is a randomized parallel clinical study to compare the effect of Asanadi ghanavati and Gomutra haritaki vati.

Asanadi ghanavati made from Asanadi gana,which is described in Astang hridaya sutra sthan adhyaya-15.

Gomutra haritaki is reference of Astang hridaya chikitsa sthan adhyaya -8

Patients were assessed before and after treatment by laboratory parameters -lipid profile.
