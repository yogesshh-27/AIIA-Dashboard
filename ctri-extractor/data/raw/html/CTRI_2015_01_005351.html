Intervention / Comparator Agent
Modification(s)  
Type  Name  Details 
Comparator Agent  Placebo 500mg twice per day for a period of six months  Placebo capsule were prepared (by using dicalcium phosphate-300mg, starch-100mg, calcium carbonate-100mg) similar to test drug and administered in similar manner to test drug. Both normal elderly cases as well as diagnosed cases of senile dementia of Alzheimer?s type were treated with the test formulation and another group with placebo as per schedule. 
Intervention  Test formulation 500mg twice per day for a period of six months  500mg capsule was prepared with the help of additive and effective dose of each plant extract was determined during pre-clinical studies. The test formulation was given in two divided doses 1 capsule morning and 1 capsule evening (8am after breakfast and 8 pm after meals) The dried whole plant of Bacopa monnieri and Centella asiatica, root of Withania somnifera and fruits of Hippophae rhamnoides were utilized to obtain hydro-methanolic extract. The extract of each plant was utilized for identification of active compound present in each plant. For this purpose chromatographic separation were done by using TLC, HPLC and HPTLC. After determination of active compound the molecular characterization were done with the help of IR and NMR.

Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

When the hydro-alcoholic extract of Bacopa monnieri, Centela asiatica, Withania somnifera and Hippophae rhamnoides in effective doses was administered in combine form orally to normal elderly as well as SDAT patients showed improvement in cognitive function, as mini-mental state examination scores, memory and attention span scores were found to be improved and no further decline was noticed. The test formulation exerted neuro-modulatory activity by regulating 5-HT and nor-adrenaline, reducing inflammatory markers like IL-6, TNF-Î± and acting on oxidative stress markers. An overall improvement in mental health status by improving memory and cognition, sleep, psychotic behavior and general feeling of well being was observed among senile dementia of Alzheimerâ€™s type patients. Further, the test formulation slowed down the process of cognitive decline in normal elderly people. The test formulation is proposed as a better remedial measure for the prevention and management of cognitive deficits particularly memory problems among elderly people and also in various types of neurodegenerative disorders.
