Publication Details    
Brief Summary   The study is randomised control clinical study to evaluate efficacy of dhatri lauha tablet in group A  and group B dhatri lauha tablet with kostha shudhi and deepanapachana with hareethaki choorna and shuntichoorna respectively  group C control group of iron and folic acid tablet. The primary out come measures the changes in subsidence of clinical signs and symptoms measured at baseline, haematology report showing hb greater than 11.5g/dl  and absence of recurrence during follow up. the study period is for 3 months  and dhatrilauha will be given in dosage of 500mg 2 tab once daily in early morning empty stomach with madhu and ghritha in unequal proportion.Secondary outcome measures are changes in general health conditions after 3months
