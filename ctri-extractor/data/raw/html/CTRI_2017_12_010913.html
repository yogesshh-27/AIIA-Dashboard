Brief Summary
Modification(s)  
Chronic liver disease in the clinical context is a disease process of the liver that involves a process of progressive destruction and regeneration of the liver parenchyma leading to fibrosis and cirrhosis. Major causes of morbidity and mortality worldwide. Many chronic liver disorders have no cure, many people have been attracted to alternative health care. Herbal supplements are widely used  in chronic liver disease by many people. Many experimental studies also proved various role of ayurvedic drugs in protecting liver function. Limitations of present modern medical management. Data from this study will be used to calculate the sample size and systematically plan the prospective clinical study. The objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy of Herbo-metalic ayurvedic formulations in treating patients with chronic liver failure.
