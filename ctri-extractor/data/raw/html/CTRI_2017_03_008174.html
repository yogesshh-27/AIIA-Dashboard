Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

Total 44 Thalassemic patients were registered for this study as 22 in each group A and B. out of them 1 patient in group A was discontinued due to various reasons and rest 43 patients completed the course. Group A patients were given the trial drugs i.e. Modified Musta-Triphaladi Avaleha and Gandhakadi Yoga Tablets along with the modern medical management. With anupana Ushnodaka and doses as per youngâ€™s formula. While group B was the control group where patients were undergone only modern medical management (blood transfusion and iron chelation). Treatment period and follow-up were 12 weeks each and subjective and objective parameters of all patients were assessed before and after the treatment.After 12 weeks of treatment, overall effect of therapy showed that, in Group A, 10 (47.61%) patients got maximum improvement, 6(28.57%) patients got moderate improvement and 5 (23.80%) patients got mild improvement. No improvement was not seen in any patient. In Group B none of the patients got maximum improvement, moderate improvement, mild improvement and in all 22 patients (100%) got no improvement.

On comparing subjective, objective parameters, BT Interval and general health status, trial group was found to be far better than control group. Modified Musta-Triphaladi Avaleha and Gandhakadi Yoga have effective role as an adjuvant along with the conventional approach in the management of Thalassemia Major.
