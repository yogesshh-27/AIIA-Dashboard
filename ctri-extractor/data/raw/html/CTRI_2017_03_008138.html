Brief Summary
Modification(s)  		 Garbhini chhardi(vomiting in pregnancy-emesis gravidarum) is common complaint during the first half of the pregnancy.
It can be seen in 50-80% patient.
One should take care to treat this in initial stage and prevent complication.
So there is great scope for research to find out potent and effective drug of Ayurveda for the management of garbhini chhardi(vomiting in pregnancy-emesis gravidarum)
It is an open,randomized,controlled,comparative clinical study of chhardi ripu vati in the management of garbhini chhardi
