Brief Summary  

Prameha is a Tridoshajavyadhi but the predominance of Dosha and Dooshya enables its classification into Vataja, Pitaja & Kaphaja Pramehas. Major symptoms of Prameha (Prediabetes) are Prabhuta Mutrata (Polyuria), Avila Mutrata (Turbidity in urine), Mutra Madhurya, Pipasa - Adhika (Polydipsia), Kshudha- Adhika ((polyphagia), Swedadhikya (Perspiration), Karapadadaha( burning sensation in palm and sole), Alasya/Utsahahani (lethargy/ lack of enthusiasm) and Nidradhikya ( excessive Sleep).  Prediabetes is one of the major clinically entity, striking resemblance with that of Prameha and Diabetes Mellitus having close resemblance with Madhumeha. Lifestyle and dietary errors are the major etiological categories described for Prameha, which closely resemble with the etiology of Prediabetes. In Prameha Chikitsa Acharyas have specifically emphasized on use of Shodhana Chikitsa. Vamana Karma (therapeutic emesis) and Virechana Karma (therapeutic purgation) are indicated in Sthula Pramehi. Variuos Aushad Yogas are also mentioned in the classics for the management of Prameha. Nidana Parivarjana and Pathya Aahar Vihar have a significant role in the prevention and management of Prameha/ Prediabetes. The study will conducted on 90 clinically diagnosed & confirmed cases of Prameha / Prediabetes from OPD & IPD section of National institute of Ayurveda Jaipur and community screening. The patients will be equally and randomly divided into three groups with 30 patients each Viz Group. A (Vamana Karma with Madanphala Yoga and Virechana with Abhayaadi modaka), Group.B (Darvayadi Ghanavati Yoga) and Group C: Lifestyle modification. Written consent will be taken from the patient before starting trial. Assessment will be done before treatment, after treatment and during last follow up.  Observations and results of the treatments will be compared and statistically tested. Conclusion will be drawn on the basis of observations and finding of statistical analysis.
