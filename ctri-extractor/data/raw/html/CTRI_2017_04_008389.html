Brief Summary  

Hypothesis- Silk hair of corn (Makkaya) kshara is effective in the management of cholelitheasis

Cholelithiasis (gall bladder stone) is a crystalline concretion formed within the gall bladder by accretion of bile components. A study shows that more than 85% of people having gall stones will be asymptomatic even for years. 

Presence of  stones in gall bladder, either symptomatic or asymptomatic may lead to acute or chronic cholecystitis and presence of stones in other parts of biliary tract can cause obstruction of bile ducts, which can lead to serious conditions as ascending cholangitis or pancreatitis. Any of these conditions can be life threatening and are considered to be surgical emergencies and require compulsory cholecystectomy.

The drugs to dissolve gall stones or other non surgical methods are still considered experimental in modern medicine. By over all view it is very much clear that treatment  prescribed in modern medicine for Cholelithiasis is far from satisfactory and is often accompanied with several side effects. So there is large scope in Ayurveda in the field of management of Cholelithiasis. Acharya Sushruta very specifically indicates Paneeya Kshara in the management of Ashmari and the Kshara due to its ksharana property can easily dissolve Ashmari. silk hair of  Makkaya is having  Diuresis and Kaliuresis effect. Hence in Cholelithiasis, considering it as Pittashayagata Ashmari, use of Paneeya Kshara prepared out of Silk hair of corn (Makkaya)being made here is expected to contribute towards better management of Cholelithiasis
