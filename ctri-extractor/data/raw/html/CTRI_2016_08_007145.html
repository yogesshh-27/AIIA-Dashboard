Publication Details    
Brief Summary
Modification(s)  		 Patient of Non Healing ulcer i.e. Diabetic/ Venous/ arterial ulcers  and other ulcers which not heals after 4 weeks will be included in this study. Selected patients will be randomly divided into 2 Groups. Group A (n=50) will be treated with local application of Thumari(Securinega leucopyrus) Gel and Group B (n=30) will be treated with local application of Povidone iodine ointment. dressing will be done daily in the morning for 30 days. Assessment will be done weekly interval to access pain, discharge, size etc. Follow up will be done for one month. Observed finding will be recorded in special research proforma. Final conclusion will be drawn on the basis of reported result after completing stydy.
