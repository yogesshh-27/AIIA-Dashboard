Brief Summary   Pregnancy is a unique exciting and joyous time in a womanâ€™s life as it highlights womanâ€™s amazing creative and nurturing power Growing fetus depends entirely on its mother for all its needs So pregnant women must take measures to remain healthy and well nourished to have a healthy child Certain physiological changes take place during pregnancy and Garbhini Chardi or Emesis gravidarum is one among them
Emesis gravidarum is a common obstetrical problem seen in First trimester of pregnancy in about 50 percent  of pregnant women Nausea and vomiting tend to be worsening in the morning and so termed as Morning Sickness and frequently continues throughout the day Altered hormonal and immunological status is considered responsible for initiation of the manifestations which is probably aggravated by the neurological factors For such physiological alterations if proper care is not given it may leads to complications like severe dehydration tiredness weight loss etc which may affect mother and growing fetus So one should take care to treat this condition in initial stage and prevent complications

        Systematic supervision examination and advice of a woman during pregnancy is called antenatal prenatal care Antenatal care comprises of careful history taking and examinations (general and obstetrical) and advice given to the pregnant woman Objectives of anti natal care are to assess the health status of the mother and fetus, to screen out the at risk pregnancy and to formulate the plan of subsequent management to obtain a base line information against which the subsequent changes are assessed and which are of importance in the determination of the gestational age Antinatal advice given for diet immunisation for Tetanus Toxoid

        Acharyas have explained Chardi as one of the Vyakta Garbha Lakshanas of garbhini According to Ayurvedic classics there are eight Garbhopadravas in which Garbhini Chardi is one of them Due to non fulfillment of Dauhruda and presence of growing fetus Vata gets provoked resulting in vomiting                                          

       For its management assessment of relative predominance of various Doshas should be made Keeping this in view an attempt has been made to control Garbhini Chardi with Bilwa Laja Yoga Properties of this medicine are Deepana Pachana Grahi Balya Tridoshara Raktaprasadana This formulation is being modified as a syrup form which can be easily prepared and administered. Keeping these views in mind this medicine is opted for the present study
