Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

This clinical study seeks to evaluate therapeutic efficacy of a pure herbal formulation of 11 raw plants parts known to be useful in diabetes and lowering of blood sugar, in a population of patients in Varanasi with diabetes. 

Factors of patient adherence are explored over 3 (three) months.

Medium-term blood sugar measured by HbA1c is the primary biochemical outcome measure.

The herbal formulation contains unripe mango seed (Mangifera indica), karela fruit (Momordica charantia), jamun seed (Syzygium cuminii), neem seed (Azadirachta indica), onion seed (Allium cepa), babul fruit (Acacia nilotica), bala seed (Sida cordifolia), fenugreek seed (Trigonella foenum-gracum), gurmar leaf/stem (Gymnema sylvestre), turmeric rhizome (Curcuma longa), and haritaki fruit pulp (Terminalia chebula).
