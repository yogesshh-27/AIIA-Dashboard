Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

National AYUSH Mission (NAM) of Ministry of AYUSH, Govt. of India states Operational Guidelines for AYUSH Services wherein it has clearly mentioned the Core/essential activities viz. Co-location of AYUSH facilities at PHCs, CHCs and District Hospitals, Upgradation of Government AYUSH Hospitals, AYUSH Dispensaries, Public outreach activity and Behavioural Change Communication (BCC)/ Information Education and Communication etc.1

Behaviour change communication (BCC) is widely recognized as one of the main health promotion strategies. It is an interactive process of working with individuals and communities to develop communication strategies to promote positive behaviours as well as create a supportive environment to enable them to adopt and sustain positive behaviours. The primary objectives of BCC in public health programmes, are to modify lifestyles which risk individual well-being, and to achieve health-improvements and environmental change.2

India is shifting from communicable diseases to non- communicable diseases (NCDs).  Early prevention and case detection is the most important strategy of all non-communicable diseases including cancer which are generally emerged due to lifestyle deviations and ill-healthy diet. Disease prevention, promotion of health and specific intervention considering patient and environmental and dietary factor are the strengths of AYUSH systems. NAM support for BCC strategy incorporating AYUSH strengths in early prevention of such NCDs which will be advocated by the health providers (AYUSH physicians).1

Cancer is a major component of morbidity and mortality in India and world over. Cancer, Precancerous states have multidimensional presentations and modes of development and spread. The current research findings have brought to light that Cancer is both preventable and treatable provided the physicians and common people have clear understanding about how cancer is caused, how to identify it in the earliest possible form and how to choose correct treatment modalities. Ayurvedic Faculty is a strong health care force spread across the length and breadth of India and as said earlier Government of India has recognized the sheer potential of this group and has taken initiatives to collocate them along with Allopathy dispensaries, hospital across country under schemes like NRHM.  It is also a well-known fact that most of the rural India the Ayurvedic physicians play a vital role in delivering health care services. Ayurvedic graduates are trained in both traditional and modern methods of diagnosis and are expected to identify any precancerous or cancerous condition in its very early forms.

BCC is the comprehensive process which involves various steps and involves various stakeholders including healthcare providers in the community in it. A Knowledge Attitude Practice (KAP) survey is a representative study of a specific population to collect information on what is known, believed and done in relation to a particular topic. In most KAP surveys, data are collected using a structured, standardized questionnaire. KAP survey data are essential to help plan, implement and evaluate Advocacy, Communication and social mobilisation (ACSM) work. A survey could also be designed to explore ways to involve all health providers including outpatient government services, hospitals, and NGOs. The data collected enable programme managers to set programme priorities, to estimate resources required for various activities, to select the most effective communication channels and messages, to establish baseline levels and measure change that results from interventions, and for advocacy (e.g. to show the magnitude of a challenge, which in turn, may inform resource needs).3

Various KAP studies have been carried out in India and abroad on various themes and cancer also. However as per published literature, any KAP study is not conducted in Cancer and Ayurvedic fraternity hence it becomes essential to conduct this study. The survey data will serve as baseline data which will help in designing the BCC strategy for Cancer and its better implementation in the community.
