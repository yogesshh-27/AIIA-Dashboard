Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

Both trial drugs Gandhkadiyoga agatsya patrabhavit and GandhkadiYoga Shigru patrabhavit shown very promising result in subjective and ojective criteria, both deacresed serum iron and serum feritin and incresed serum TIBC and Hb . also decresed SGOT and SGPT. Both trial drugs increased B.T. interval   2 to 58 days. in group A maximum 12 days and In group B maximum 58 days. Also improve in qaulity of life of thalassemia patients.
