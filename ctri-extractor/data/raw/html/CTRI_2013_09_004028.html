Brief Summary
Modification(s)  

Present study entitled Concept of Healthy living as mentioned in Ayurvedic classics in relation to Lifestyle disorders (A conceptual and survey study) was carried out with following

 

aims and objectives

To explain Healthy living as mentioned in Ayurvedic classics that which may be suitable for present era.

A survey study to identify the factors related with life-style in the causation of Sthaulya and Madhumeha.

Introduction

In the Introduction the research subject Concept of healthy living as mentioned in Ayurvedic classics in relation to lifestyle disorders has been introduced with its need and importance. The present scenario of lifestyle disorders and the potential of Ayurveda in its prevention were specified.  The previous work done in similar topics in various institutions of India were also mentioned.

Survey Study

Madhumaha (Type-2 DM)

The present survey study was cross-sectional survey study undertaken to understand the lifestyle related factors of the Madhueha. Total 150 patients of Madhumeha diagnosed with in one year, visiting to the OPD/IPD of IPGT & RA, Jamnagar-Gujarat were surveyed.

Observations

In the present survey study maximum patients were male (60%), belonging to 41-50 years age group (40%), Married (97.33%), Hindu (88.67%), primary education (31.33%), middle socio-economic status (66.67%) and had sedentary occupation. Maximum patients were having BMI between 25-29.99 (47.34%), maximum patients were having Negative family history (72%) and Chronicity > 8-12 month (41.34%).  Maximum patients (58%) reported that they were often taking food when feel good hunger and up to full of stomach (53.33%) and were feeling heaviness after eating. Maximum Patients (56.66%) were reported that they only sometimes pay attention to their hunger level and maximum patients (57.34) used to take food just because it is the time to have food.  Majority of the patients (45.34%) reported to take deep fried/ sweet items sometimes in breakfast. Maximum Patients (57.33%) were often taking food containing more oil/ghee/butter. Maximum patients (68%) reported to take more salty food items sometimes and 51.33% patients reported to like more sour taste food items. Maximum patients (47.34%) were taking milk and milk products more than six times in a week, intake of Ghee more than six times in a week was found in 53.67% of patients. Maximum numbers of patients (57.34%) were taking oily and fried food 3-5 times in a week. Intake of potato as vegetables was found very popular and Maximum patients (67.33%) were found to take potato more than 3-5 times in a week. In present survey study it was found that 60.67% patients were never doing exercise or going for brisk walk in the morning/evening, 89.34% patients were never doing Yoga Asana etc. It was found that 28.66% patients often and 26.67% patients almost always sleep in day time. 67.33% of patients sometimes suppress natural urge of defecation, urination etc.  In present survey study it was found that 57.34% patients are not at all happy considering all the things in life, 59.34% patients no more than usual enjoying their day to day activities. Maximum patients (65.33%) were found to feel lazy for doing work more than usual.

Sthaulya (Obesity)

Cross-sectional survey study on Sthaulya was undertaken to understand the lifestyle related factors involved in the causation of disease. Total 250 Sthaulya patients were surveyed visiting to the OPD/IPD of IPGT & RA, Jamnagar-Gujarat.

Observations

In the present survey study of Sthaulya (Obesity) maximum patients were female (83.2%), belonging to 31-40 years age group (34.8%), Married (87.2%), Hindu (80.8%), secondary level education (34.4%), middle socio-economic status (79.6%) and had sedentary type of occupation. Maximum patients (45.6%) were having BMI between 30-34.9; maximum patients were having Negative family history (48.8%) and Chronicity > 5 years (54.8%). In present Survey study it was found that maximum 60% patients were often taking food when feel good hunger and 44.4% patients were sometimes taking food just because it is the time to have food, 50.4% patients were often eating up to full of stomach and feel heaviness after eating. Most of the patients (44.8%) were sometimes taking deep fried food/sweets/non-veg in their breakfast. It was found that 56.8% of patients were often taking food containing more oil, ghee or butter and 51.2% of patients were almost always drinking water immediately after having food. 27.2% patients more than 6 times and 27.2% 3-5 times were taking milk and milk products, 53.6% of patients were taking ghee more than 6 times in a week. It was found that maximum 52.8% of patients were taking oily and fried food 3-5 times in a week. Use of potatoes as vegetables was found very popular and 63.6% patients were taking Potatoes 3-5 times in a week. In present survey it was found that 61.6% patients were never doing any exercise or going for brisk walk in morning/evening time, 84% patients were never doing Yoga, Asana etc. and 92% patients were reported to never participating actively in sportive or physical activities. It was reported that 35.6% of patients were almost always and 24.8% of patients were often, sleeping in day time, 33.2% patients almost always and 25.6% were often sleeping immediately after having food in day time. Maximum patients (42.8%) were enjoying sleeping for long time in morning and maximum patients (56.8%) were reported to rarely wake up before 6 am.  In present survey study it was found that 46% of patients were feeling happy no more than usual and 32% patients were not at all happy considering all the things in life. 66.8% patients were feeling lazy for doing work more than usual.

Discussion

Madhumeha

Data of 150 surveyed Madhumeha (type-2 DM) patients was analysed by calculating percentage to determine the lifestyle related factors responsible for the manifestation of disease. Obtained results from survey were discussed in detail. In present survey study maximum patients (72%) were having negative family history it strongly suggest that sedentary lifestyle, faulty food habits and psychological distress were the chief etiological factors behind manifestation of Madhumeha.

Sthaulya

Total 250 Sthaulya patients were surveyed and the data was analysed by calculating percentage to determine the main lifestyle related factors involved in the causation of the diseases. In Gujarat state, especially in Jamnagar area it was found that people are less physically active and take food rich in fat and sugar, and if more calories are consumed than expended, the excess calories are stored as fat. Such lifestyle pattern is definitely a cause of weight gain and Sthaulya. All the lifestyle related factors were discussed in detail with supportive classical and modern references.

Conclusion

From present survey study of Madhumeha and Sthaulya it can be concluded that intake of heavy, fatty food, physical inactivity, habit of day sleep and psychological imbalance are the main factors associated in the manifestation of disease.
