Publication Details    
Brief Summary  

This is a cross-sectional study to assess the association of complementary alternative medicine (CAM) with patients satisfaction in Breast cancer conducted in Tata Memorial Hospital, Parel, Mumbai, India.

This study is an attempt to know more about the use of CAM in Breast cancer, also about extent of use, patients’ satisfaction, effectiveness of CAM, and cost in treatment of Breast cancer.
