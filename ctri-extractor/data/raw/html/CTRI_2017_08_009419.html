Brief Summary  

Unacceptable taste of oral medication has been the challenge to the medical fraternity since time immemorial, more so with Ayurvedic system of medicine. Oral administration of bitter medicated ghee/oil in increased dosage pattern (Snehapana) as a part of detox treatment in Ayurveda system of medicine has been major hurdle in clinical practice. However, this procedure yielding promising results, Ayurvedic practitioners seldom or never compromise with the concept of Snehapana procedure. This particular factor, many a time leads to the rejection of medication or less acceptance of Ayurveda system of medicine among public or patients.

Nano-encapsulation of this dosage form of medicated ghee would resolve the incompatibility and bring palatability and acceptability. To rule out the possibilities of loss of bioactive agents in the process of nano-ecapsulation, a comparative clinical evaluation of the same will be done with conventional medication in the management of selective dermatological disorders.
