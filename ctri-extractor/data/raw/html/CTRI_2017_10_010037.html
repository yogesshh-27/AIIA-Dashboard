Details  1) Adolescent girls aged between 12-15years.

ExclusionCriteria 
Details  1) Adolescent girls suffering from major systemic illness necessitating long term treatment.
2) Adolescent girls with evidence of malignancy.
3) Adolescent girls with concurrent serious hepatic dysfunction (defined as AST and/or ALT>3 times of the upper normal limit) or renal dysfunction (defined as S. creatinine>1.2mg/dl) uncontrolled pulmonary dysfunction (asthmatic and COPD patients)
4) Co-morbidity like TB, UTI and bleeding disorders etc.
5) H/o hypersensitivity to any of the trial drug or their ingredients.
6) Adolescent girls who have completed participation in any other clinical trial during the past six months.

Brief Summary  

The present clinical study entitled â€œStudy of Prevalence of Iron Deficiency Anemia in Adolescent Girls and Efficacy of Vajravatak Mandoor in Its Managementâ€ with the following aims and objectives-

1.      To screen adolescent girls between 12-15 years of age for the prevalence of anemia.

2.      To study its co-relation with the socio-economic status of the family.

To evaluate the safety and efficacy of an Ayurveda compound Vajravatak Mandoor  in iron deficiency anemia

CLINICAL STUDY

The present research trial has been undertaken with the following methods-

METHODS

Study type: The clinical study was conducted in the form of an interventional, randomized control trial, open label, grouped (group A & group B).

End point: Safety and Efficacy

Number of patients to be completed in the clinical trial (sample size): 100

 

Timelines

Total trial period: 8 weeks

Washout/Preparatory Period: 4 weeks (if required)

Follow up period: 4 weeks

Statistical Analysis: 8 weeks

The study was conducted in two phases:

Â·         Phase I: Cross sectional demographic study of prevalence of anemia in adolescent girls in Jaipur district by survey method.

Â·         Phase II: Clinical Trial

 

Selection of Cases

Source: Health checkup camps: Cross sectional study was conducted from july 2015 to January 2016 by attending health checkup camps at various areas situated in Jaipur district.

Subjects attended the O.P.D. and I.P.D. of Kaumarbhritya Department of National Institute of Ayurveda, Jaipur and from various schools situated in Jaipur by survey method were screened for the present study.

Age Group: Girls of 12-15 years of age were selected for the study.

Number of Cases: 100 cases were planned to include in the clinical trial. Total 122 cases were enrolled out of which 22 cases dropped out. Study was conducted on 100 cases. (50 in each group)
