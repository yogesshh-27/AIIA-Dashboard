SUMMARY

Total 31 patients were registered and randomly divided into two groups, Group A and Group B. Out of the 15 patients registered in Group A, 14 completed the study and 1 left against medical advice. In Group B, total 16 Patients were registered, out of which 15 completed the trial, and 1 patient could not continue the treatment.

Though both the groups were statistically insignificant on objective parameters, but Group A (Virechana followed by Shamana) showed better results on subjective parameters than Group B (Shamana).

Looking at the chronic nature and gradual onset of the disease, sufficient duration is required to cure Ama and Agnimandya at Dhatu level. Results could have been more effective if the trial would have been continued for longer duration.

Hence, Null hypothesis is rejected and alternative hypothesis is accepted.

Virechana followed by Shamana therapy (Triphladya Guggulu and Punarnavadi Kashaya) is more effective in the management of Hypothyroidism than Shamana therapy alone (Triphladya Guggulu and Punarnavadi Kashaya).
