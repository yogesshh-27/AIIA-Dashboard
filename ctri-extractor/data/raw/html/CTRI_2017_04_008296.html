Primary Outcome
Modification(s)  
Outcome  TimePoints 
1.Pain reduction in Kashtartava by by using VMS- Verbal multidimensional scoring system.
2.Changes in Haematological parameters.
3.Improvement in associated symptoms like Alpaartava, Prasek, Chhardi, Vibhandha, Shrama, Aruchi, Vankshana Shoola, Kati-Janu Shoola.
4.For subjective parameters especially on Ayurvedic parlance suitable scoring pattern shall be developed. 		 all outcome will be observed every month. and final outcome will be observed after 2 months of administration of medicines 
 
Secondary Outcome  
Outcome  TimePoints 
The total effect of the therapy will be assessed considering to the overall improvement in signs and symptoms. For this purpose, following categories will be maintained.
1.Complete remission: 76%-100% relief in the signs and symptoms will be considered as complete resmission.
2.Marked improvement: 51%-75% relief in the signs and symptoms.
3.Improved: 26%-50% relief
4.Unchanged:Below 25% relief. 		 Follow up after 1 month during treatment and after completion of treatment for 2 cycles.
