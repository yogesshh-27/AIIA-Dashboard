Publication Details    
Brief Summary   The study was to evaluate the efficacy  of Yava kshara taila uttara basti in group A   and Kumari taila uttara basti in Group B and to compare their effect in Tubal Blockage. Patients having at least one sided Tubal Blockage were selected for the trial.After cessation of menses,5 ml Uttara Basti for six days with interval of three days in between was given for two consecutive menstrual cycles.The effect of therapy was assessed on the basis of patency of fallopian tube confirmed by HSG or on the basis of conception.
