Brief Summary   This is a randomized parallel study to evaluate the efficacy of Draksha dadimadi syrup in one group, while in another group Drakshadi syrup will be given. Both syrup will be given for 90 days and dose fixed according to sharangdhar samhita. both syrup will be given for iron deficiency anemia in 5 to 12 years age group children. Results will be assessed through laboratory parameters like Hb and subjective improvement in clinical signs and symptoms.
