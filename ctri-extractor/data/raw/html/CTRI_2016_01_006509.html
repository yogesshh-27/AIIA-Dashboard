Brief Summary   Hair fall is a psycho somatic disorder.It has cosmetic value also. In the  present days young people also suffering from hair falls. Artificial methods implied for hair growth are expensive and injurious. A natural and safe hair grower is the need of the hour. It is hoped that  Gunja will stand as an answer for hair fall.

This study is randomized ,parallel group trail comprising the safety and efficacy of Gunja mula paste as external application and Gunja mula thaila  as application application on effected area and Gunja mula thiala  as nasal drops 4 to 5 drops per  week

patients are divided in to 3 groups
Group 1: Gunja mula paste as local application depending upon  the ares  effected
Group2 : Gunja mula thiala extranal appliction as per the area effected
Group 3: Gunja mula thiala as pratimarsa nasya 4 to 5 drops per week in 40days
