It bridges traditional Ayurvedic trial workflows (Ayush endpoints, Prakriti profiling, Panchakarma interventions) with modern international healthcare informatics standards (FHIR & CDISC ODM).

4. Key Design Principles & Reliability

Local-First Durability

Embedded SQLite database ensures instant zero-latency queries and full offline availability for remote field research sites without internet dependency.

Asynchronous Performance

Python FastAPI handles simultaneous clinical data submissions without blocking, maintaining sub-10ms response times for all analytics endpoints.

Standards Compliance

Native CDISC and HL7 FHIR export schemas allow seamless integration with ICMR, CTRI, and international clinical trial registries.

5. Security, Audit Trail & Interoperability

Clinical Integrity Guarantee: AYURCTMS enforces strict immutability on patient adverse event reports and audit entries. All schema transactions adhere to 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records guidance with automated timestamps, user role verification, and deterministic data serialization.
